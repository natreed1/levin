                            Privacy & data

                            Understand where your information lives.

                            Your Flyleaf data

                            Account details, teams (rooms), and messages are stored in Flyleaf’s database. Research sessions, events, and artifacts are kept in a separate directory for your account. Team Graph runs use the team owner’s ledger and model links. Invited people get Viewer or Editor access (Docs-style); Editors can edit graphs, hire agents, and change room config. Only the owner can delete the team or manage who has access.

                            Passwords are hashed. Connected model keys are encrypted before being written to disk. Flyleaf does not send ledger content to a model unless you explicitly start a model-backed action.
